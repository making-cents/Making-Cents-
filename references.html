References & Further Reading

Every lesson on Making Cents is built around evidence that you can explore for yourself. Primary sources are the research, statistics and institutional material used to underpin the lesson. Further reading & watching is deliberately more accessible: short explainers, articles and videos that explore the same economic question from another angle.
