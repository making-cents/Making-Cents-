References & further reading.

Every lesson is designed to explain economics in plain English, but the ideas should remain traceable to evidence. This page collects the principal sources behind the lessons and a small selection of further reading for anyone who wants to go deeper.

How to use this page: “Sources” are the principal evidence bases used in the lesson. “Further reading” is included to help you explore the economics beyond Making Cents. External sites open in a new tab.
